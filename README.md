Same brand, same butter-heavy ingredient philosophy, two different jobs. The size difference matters more than you'd think: 20 g of mask against 10 g of balm.

## Cost per gram, which changes the decision

Divide the price by the weight and the recommendation gets simpler.

- Lip Sleeping Mask: $24 for 20 g = **$1.20 per gram**
- Lip Glowy Balm: $19 for 10 g = **$1.90 per gram**

The mask is the cheaper product per gram by a wide margin, and it's also the one a single jar lasts longest, because you use a thin layer rather than reapplying all day. If your actual problem is dry, flaky lips in winter, the mask does more work per dollar. The balm is the one you buy for the format — pocket-sized, glossy, easy to reapply — and you pay a premium for that format.

There's also a practical overlap. The mask can be used in the morning as a thin layer under lipstick, and the balm can go on before bed if you can't stand a heavy layer. Neither product has a hard rule against it. The formulas are close cousins; the difference is mostly texture and how much you apply.
